# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Developers and AI power users who want to turn diverse content (YouTube, docs, code) into reusable agent skills. They value efficiency, clarity, and non-generic, high-quality output.

## Product Purpose
AnythingToSkill exists to lower the barrier to creating high-quality, installable agent skills. It automates the fetching, analysis, and formatting of knowledge into the universal skills.sh format.

## Brand Personality
Minimalist, technical, differentiated. It avoids the "glowing AI" clichés in favor of a sharp, high-density, and helpful aesthetic.

## Anti-references
Generic AI SaaS templates (gradients, glowing icons, soft-and-round), "AI slop" copy (empower/unleash/supercharge), and interfaces that prioritize decoration over detail.

## Design Principles
1. **Technical Clarity**: Avoid the "AI monoculture" look. Use sharp edges, high-contrast typography, and purposeful density.
2. **Utility First**: Value is carried by the quality of the generated skill and the transparency of the process, not by decorative effects.
3. **Efficiency**: Design for a fast flow. The user's time is precious; the interface should reflect that.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. High contrast is a priority given the technical nature and minimalist dark theme.
